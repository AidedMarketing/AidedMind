# v35 — Topic care and visible connection reviews

In More → Appearance → Manage topics, choose a topic to rename it, split selected pieces into a new topic, or merge it with another. Rename retains the topic's map identity and remembers old names. Split keeps the original topic available for future captures. Undo restores the last action during the current session, preserving later content and separate topic edits. Later captures in a renamed or merged topic also inherit its restored names.

Review library connections now shows saved progress, completed/checking/queued/failed counts, Pause/Resume and Retry failed. A paused review stays paused after reopening. An in-progress check may finish after pausing. Checks use model tokens, with at most three automatic attempts per piece; summaries are not regenerated. Keep the app open while it works. Multiple tabs serialize connection checks with Web Locks where supported.

No automatic reclassification or model calls run because of this update. Actual category quality still needs a sample of the real library. Broader semantic candidate retrieval remains a separate improvement.
